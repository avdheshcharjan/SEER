# Market Ideas – 24-Hour Binary Questions

These questions are intended for parimutuel markets with a **YES/NO** outcome and a maximum 24-hour resolution window.

---

## 🪙 Crypto
1. **Bitcoin Close Above $65,000?**  
   Will BTC/USD close **above $65,000** on the next UTC daily candle?

2. **Ethereum 5% Move?**  
   Will ETH/USD trade **±5%** from its current price (at market creation) within the next 24 hours?

---

## 🌍 Current Affairs
3. **Major Earthquake Reported?**  
   Will the USGS report a **magnitude ≥6.0 earthquake** anywhere in the world in the next 24 hours?

4. **OPEC Emergency Statement?**  
   Will OPEC release an **unscheduled public statement** about oil production cuts within the next 24 hours?

---

## 🗳 Politics
5. **White House Press Briefing?**  
   Will the White House hold an **unscheduled press briefing** today (local Washington, D.C. time)?

6. **UK Parliament Confidence Vote Triggered?**  
   Will a **no-confidence motion** be officially tabled in the UK Parliament within 24 hours?

---

## 💻 Tech
7. **Apple Product Leak Confirmed?**  
   Will a **verified Apple executive or PR representative** confirm any **new, unreleased hardware** via media or Apple.com within the next 24 hours?

8. **Major AI Outage?**  
   Will any of the top 3 public LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini) report a **global service outage ≥1 hour** in the next 24 hours?

---

## ⚽ Sports
9. **Champions League Upset?**  
   Will any **underdog team (odds ≥3.0 pre-match)** win their UEFA Champions League match today?

10. **NBA Triple-Double Tonight?**  
    Will **any NBA player** record a **triple-double** in today’s scheduled games?

---
*Each market should clearly specify the reference time zone (e.g., UTC) and data source for resolution (official price feeds, government sites, league stats, etc.).*
